? How to use the Mr Fix-It

Fix Finder: type your problem in plain words. The repair field is detected automatically — use the dropdown to override it. Tap 🔧 Get my fix for the best archived match with ranked solutions.

Photo / video: attach a photo or video of the problem for review. Files stay on your device only — never uploaded. Limit 100 MB per file; damaged files show a clear message.

Fix Archive: every record has a permanent JAH-FIX-###### ID. Browse the full archive A–Z — by repair field or by title — or search here, filter by field chip, page with Prev/Next, or open a record directly with ?fix=JAH-FIX-000001. Results labeled ARCHIVE RECORD come from the archive.

Live Fix Cam: point your camera at the problem and follow the walkthrough beside your live feed. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. If the camera is blocked or missing, you'll get a plain-language message explaining why and what to do.

Ask the Fix-It AI: grounded in the archive — it never invents fixes and says NOT FOUND when nothing matches. Web findings are labeled ONLINE RESULT, always separate from ARCHIVE RECORDs.

My Fixes: your progress, saved on this device only. Check off diagnosis steps on any record; mark it fixed when done. Export a JSON backup, import it back, or clear it. Nothing leaves your browser.

Safety: every record carries a LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH safety rating with warnings. HIGH-safety electrical, gas, or structural work should be done by a licensed professional. Fix records are generated guidance, not certified professional advice.
